What helps us help you

Include the app version, device model, iOS or iPadOS version, selected language, story title, and what happened. Screenshots are optional. Please leave out passwords, payment details and children’s personal information.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Lapivelle has no account or login requirement. Your favorites and preferences are saved locally on your device.

Can I use it offline?

Stories and audio included in your installed app can be read or played offline. Opening this website, sending email or downloading an app update requires an internet connection.

Why won’t a story play?

Check media volume, the selected audio output or Bluetooth connection, and the sleep timer. If a story says narration is being prepared, its audio is not included yet; you can read the available text. If the problem continues, tell us the language and story title.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings, then App and story language. Chinese Simplified and Traditional are separate choices. Switching languages stops the current playback so speech and subtitles do not get mixed.

How do I remove my local data?

Delete the app to remove its local preferences and favorites. Offloading the app may keep its data. Manage any device or cloud backups separately in Apple’s settings.
