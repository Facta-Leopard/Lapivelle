Privacy policy

How information is handled in the Lapivelle app, on this website and when you contact us.

What this policy covers

This policy covers the Lapivelle app, this website and support provided by Facta Leopard. Lapivelle is intended for adults to operate during quiet evening routines.

Information in the app

Lapivelle does not require an account or include third-party advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs or social features. It does not request microphone, camera, location or contacts access. The app does not ask children or other users to provide personal information.

Language, favorites, playback preferences and sleep-timer choices are stored locally. They are not sent to a developer-operated server. The app does not provide a cloud account or synchronize these preferences through our servers.

Stories and playback

Included story text, artwork and audio are used on the device. The app does not record your voice or send your reading or listening content to an AI service. Content may be provided through normal app updates.

The list and descriptions in More apps by the developer are included in the app. Opening that screen may load icons from Apple’s image servers. These image requests do not include story content or your favorites. Selecting an App Store link opens Apple’s service.

When you contact us

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any information you choose to include. We use this to answer your request, investigate an issue or handle a privacy question. Email providers process delivery and storage.

A GitHub issue is a public post, not a private support inbox. Do not include personal or sensitive information in an issue. Please use email for privacy requests.

Visiting this website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Its own privacy statement explains that processing.

We do not add advertising, analytics, tracking cookies, third-party embeds or remote fonts to this website. Language selection works through page links; we do not store a visitor profile.

GitHub’s privacy statement

Sharing and external services

We do not sell personal information or use app preferences for advertising. Apple operates App Store distribution and system backup services under its own policies. GitHub and email providers handle their services under their own terms; following an external link takes you to that provider.

Apple’s privacy policy

Retention and your choices

Local app data remains until you change it or delete the app. Device or cloud backups may retain copies according to your Apple settings; we do not receive those backups.

Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable recordkeeping duties. Email us to ask about, correct or request deletion of information you sent. We cannot access data that exists only on your device. GitHub controls retention of its hosting security records.

Questions and updates

Contact us at shteosis@gmail.com with a privacy question, including if you believe a child has sent personal information through a support channel. We will update this page and its date when our practices change.
